# GAM-68 owner rehearsal

## Build

Launch:

`E:\Data\Projects\PS-gam68-grip\Builds\GAM68\Windows\PowerliftingSimulator.exe`

If Windows SmartScreen warns, choose **More info → Run anyway** (local unsigned
review build).

## Controls

- `Enter` / `Space` — confirm menu and setup actions
- `S` or Down — descend / yield
- `W` or Up — drive up
- `A` / `D` — balance
- `C` — cycle camera (3/4 competition, side depth judge, head referee front)
- `Esc` — abort attempt
- `R` — retry

## Review path

1. Main menu → **Play → Training Gym**.
2. Select the **Competition Squat Station**.
3. Keep **25 kg** and select **Begin Setup**.
4. Select **Unrack & Walkout**.
5. Wait for the referee's **SQUAT** command.
6. Hold `S` through the descent. At legal depth the HUD says
   **LEGAL DEPTH REACHED — DRIVE UP**.
7. Hold `W` through ascent to lockout.
8. Inspect the result screen and press `R` to retry.

## What to look for

- At setup and walkout, both hands should sit on the bar with the fingers
  wrapped around the shaft; the palms should not float above or in front of it.
- The elbows should be down and back, not flared at shoulder height.
- The grip should remain on the real moving bar through descent, bottom,
  ascent and lockout. The bar is only supported by the upper-back saddle; the
  hands are presentation, not a second load path.
- The squat depth, referee verdict, bar path and failure behaviour should be
  unchanged from the accepted build.

## If something looks wrong

Capture the phase (screen recording or screenshot) and note whether the hands
separate from the bar at setup, bottom or ascent. GAM-68 remains draft until
owner visual/input acceptance; do not merge it on test results alone.
