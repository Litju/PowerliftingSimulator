# GAM-68 — Presentation-only squat grip and live upper-limb anchoring

## What changed

The accepted squat plant is unchanged: **16 physical bodies, 15 joints**, the
`SquatBarSaddle`→thorax load path, the `PoweredJointController` writer and the
referee rules are untouched. No hand–bar constraint, new Rigidbody, finger
Rigidbody, forearm DOF, scapular body, force, transform pin or solver/gain
change was added.

The player-visible defect had two measured causes:

1. **The upper-limb task was anchored in the calibration world frame.** The
   hybrid walkout moves the physical athlete backwards with the bar, but the
   virtual GAM-10 grip bar stayed at the calibration origin. At standing, the
   virtual bar was measured **0.361 m in front of** the live shaft (0.307 m at
   mid-descent). The hands were reaching for a bar that was no longer there.
2. **The visible finger bones were still in bind pose.** Even with the hands
   near the shaft, the avatar showed an open hand.

## The fix

- `SquatPhysicalAdapter.TrySolveLiveUpperLimb` re-anchors the existing
  GAM-10 task to the **realized physical shoulder joint anchors** and the
  **live bar shaft**. It feeds the same one-axis elbow / ball shoulder / ball
  wrist projection; it does not add a joint.
- `SquatReferenceUpperLimb.SolveSideForLiveBar` places the **measured palm
  surface**, not a nominal wrist point, on the shaft. It searches a bounded
  elbow swivel (existing shoulder/elbow DOFs only) for the candidate with the
  lowest wrist-orientation and hand-position residual.
- `SquatPalmSurface` stores the palm frame measured from the rendered
  `SuperHero_Male` skin: dominant hand-bone vertices, covariance, outer skin
  centroids and the palmar side. An EditMode test re-measures the mesh and
  fails if the constants drift.
- `SquatPresentationGrip` poses only the existing skinned finger bones during
  `LateUpdate`, after the physical follower. It reads the physical hand and the
  live shaft and never writes physical state. It curls the fingers around the
  shaft and relaxes if the palm is not near the bar.

## Measured result (actual gameplay attempt, 25 kg)

| Phase | Before palm gap | After palm gap | Before wrist/hand state | After wrist/hand state |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| Standing | 9.2 mm | **−0.2 mm** | open hand, palm forward | fingers wrapped, palm on shaft |
| Bottom | 21.9 mm | **+1.2 mm** | open hand, palm forward | fingers wrapped, palm on shaft |
| Ascent | — | **+0.8 mm** | open hand | fingers wrapped |
| Lockout | — | **0.0 mm** | open hand | fingers wrapped |

- The measured physical palm surface is within **0–2 mm** of the live shaft
  through standing, descent, bottom, ascent and lockout.
- The existing wrist joint realises the requested orientation with **0.0°**
  residual; shoulder demand is **0.111** of channel capacity at standing and
  **0.070** at bottom (no saturation).
- The remaining measured palm-plane-normal angle is about **60°** from the
  radial direction. The palm centre is on the shaft and the fingers wrap
  around it; this is not a gap and does not use a new DOF. It is reported
  rather than hidden.
