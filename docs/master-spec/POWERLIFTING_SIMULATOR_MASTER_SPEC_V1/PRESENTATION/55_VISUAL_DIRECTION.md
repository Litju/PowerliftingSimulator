## Creative direction

**Broadcast powerlifting meets engineering clarity.**

The athlete is real and visible, the bar feels heavy, the platform feels official, and analysis is clean and credible.
The game is stylized enough to match the Quaternius hero character, but lighting/materials/camera sell weight and contact.
It should not look like a robotics laboratory, medical simulator, debug mannequin, or mobile clicker.

## Pillars

1. **Human first:** the visible athlete occupies the visual center.
2. **Weight:** plates, bar inertia, slow grind, contact and posture communicate load.
3. **Competition:** commands, platform geometry, judges/lights, crowd and broadcast framing.
4. **Scientific honesty:** analysis overlays are precise, sparse, labeled, and never claim hidden biology.
5. **Readability:** feet, bar, joint silhouette, depth/touch/lockout remain visible.
6. **Consistency:** one visual system across training, meet, replay, and analysis.

## Palette

- charcoal/graphite venue and UI foundations;
- neutral metal/rubber/wood equipment;
- warm skin/character key light;
- cool broadcast fill;
- one electric scientific accent for analysis/path;
- white/red/blue referee lights with text/shape redundancy;
- gold only for records/championship.

Exact colors are art direction and accessibility-tested, not architecture.

## Materials

- bar steel with readable shaft/sleeves/knurl but no mirror glare;
- matte rubber competition plates with clear load coding;
- textured platform/bench/rack;
- stylized but grounded character materials;
- subtle sweat/strain response;
- venue surfaces with controlled roughness;
- no excessive bloom or wet-plastic skin.

## Lighting

Hero key/fill/rim supports body/bar silhouette. Platform is brighter than audience. Shadows anchor feet, bench, rack, and
bar. Exposure is stable across camera cuts. Rule-critical landmarks must not disappear into shadow.

## Motion language

- setup is deliberate;
- unloaded/light motion is controlled;
- heavy motion slows and trembles subtly;
- failure follows physics, not canned collapse;
- cameras/audio/VFX intensify only after physical evidence;
- replay can exaggerate temporal presentation but not geometry.

## Environments

### Training gym

Functional, quiet, legible, tutorial-friendly.

### Local meet

More banners/crowd, simple broadcast, modest venue.

### Championship

Large arena, stronger lighting, crowd density, record presentation—but same platform truth and performance budget.

Fictional brands avoid unlicensed marks. Third-party asset licenses are recorded.

## Analysis visual language

World-space paths, landmark lines, support polygons, and reference ghosts are optional overlays with legends and source
class. They never appear as permanent neon skeletons in normal play. Charts use phase/event markers and restrained grids.

## Failure visual language

Show loss of balance, stall, grip slip, bar descent, or failed lockout clearly and safely. Avoid gore, injury implication,
or slapstick. Safety support may intervene after outcome freeze.

## Scope
