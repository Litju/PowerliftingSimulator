GAM-70 Gate 9 - bar-to-leg distance: geometry diagnosis, correction attempt, controlled A/B
2026-11-17, Unity 6000.3.22f1, fresh B2 plants (DeadliftPlantWorld), shipped DeadliftAttemptRuntime,
100 kg athlete, real 75 kg bar, both rigid grips, deadlift ankle-roll DOF, Drive onset tick 301.
No gain, strength, stiffness, solver, friction, contact, collider or joint-limit value was changed.
PoweredJointController stays the only joint writer; the welded grips are untouched.

Owner observation: in the Windows game the bar is visibly too far forward through the lift,
especially at lockout.

1. Measurement (DeadliftBarLegGeometry, one definition for plan and plant)
   Surface gap = sagittal (Y-Z) distance from the bar axis to a leg capsule's core segment
   - capsule radius - shaft radius (14.5 mm = the 29 mm shaft collider). Shins and thighs
   separately, both sides. Planned: solver poses of each DeadliftLiftPath key. Realized: the
   live capsule colliders and the live shaft collider every tick. Check 1: the 3D
   shaft-capsule segment distance. Check 2: the visible skinned mesh (baked with the rig's own
   rotation-only follower, vertices whose dominant bone is a thigh or shank, inside the
   shaft's span), every 10th tick. Test: DeadliftBarProximityTests (Explicit).

3. Root cause (kinematic, in the planner and in the plant)
   The ascent planner uses the setup solver's leg parameterization: hip ball, knee hinge,
   ankle hinge. With both feet planted at the B2 42 deg toe-out, each leg is then a planar
   ankle+knee chain in its own toed-out foot plane, so each hip joint centre must lie in that
   plane. With the pelvis width fixed, the two planes allow one hip Z: -0.33 m, for every knee
   angle. The only way to stand up is to lean the straight legs back, and balance (COM over
   the feet, shoulders not ahead of the hips) then puts the bar 0.18 m off the thighs. The
   missing proximity objective is not the cause: no objective can move a pinned hip.
   The plant has the DOF that removes the pin: the owner-approved, powered, +/-15 deg ankle
   inversion/eversion channel (DeadliftAnkleRoll, Gate 1). The planner did not use it.

4. Correction (DeadliftLiftPath.BarProximity, opt-in, default OFF)
   a. The planner also uses the ankle roll (joint Z channel, composed exactly as the hold
      composes the regulator's roll offset; still one joint writer), bounded.
   b. Bar-thigh proximity preference toward the declared 6 mm clearance + 19 mm measured
      tracking allowance (finding 2a), ramped in after knee passage. The clearance stays a
      hard inequality, so the plan never rests the bar on the legs. A shin term (run 1) drove
      the bar into the shins and is off (finding 2c).
   c. Below the ramp the path equals the Gate 6 path: same coordination schedule (normalized
      by the Gate 6 lockout rise), roll held at zero, same joint speed (progress rates scaled
      to the Gate 6 46.5 deg/s), and the bar's fore-aft travel bounds the progress (3 deg/cm).
   Planned lockout bar-thigh gap: 179 mm (Gate 6) -> 34 mm (roll 4), 40 (roll 2), 51 (roll 1),
   82 mm (roll 3, near-vertical bar). Static lumbar demand at lockout drops 0.48 -> 0.16-0.25
   (bar closer to the hips).
