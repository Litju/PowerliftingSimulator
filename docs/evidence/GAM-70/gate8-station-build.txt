2. Known limitation (measured, not hidden). Explicit RUNTIME_DRIVE_ONSET_ROBUSTNESS_AB, fresh
   plants, Drive onsets 301/302/305/320/350: 4/5 good lifts with the shipped settings. Onset 302
   loses a foot near lockout (FootMoved @665, bar dropped @691). Cause: the ankle regulator
   identified at the crouched setup chatters near standing (0.08 deg/tick grounded -> 1.1-1.6
   deg/tick near lockout) and saturates. One-parameter alternatives, each A/B over the same five
   onsets and reverted: regulator output rate limit 0.3 deg/tick 0/5; slower ascent 0.20 /s 3/5;
   COM-height-scaled regulator gain 0/5; gentler arrival (0.3 /s^2) 0/5 (bar on thighs early).
   The proper fix (re-identify the ankle plant near standing) is open. A real player's attempt
   can therefore fail near lockout; the referee then calls the measured fault.
