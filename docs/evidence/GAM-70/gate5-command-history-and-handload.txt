GAM-70 Gate 5 - command-history confound, first dynamic cause, measured hand-load reference
2026-11-16, Unity 6000.3.22f1, fresh B2 plants, 100 kg athlete, real 75 kg bar, both rigid grips,
16-body topology with the deadlift ankle-roll DOF. No gain, strength, stiffness, solver, friction,
contact or joint-limit value was changed in this gate.

   A: fixed-ownership N0, Drive released at tick 600 (the Gate 4 history).
   B: identical, Drive held at full intent for 900 ticks.
   First requested-Drive divergence: tick 600. First applied-command divergence: NONE.

2. First physical departure and its mechanism (B-held trace, per tick)
   Hip/knee/trunk angles stay within ~3 deg of the setup (hip -129, knee 119) through the whole
   collapse: the 500 mm backward COM travel is a rotation of the athlete+bar system about the
   foot edges, not a joint-space motion.
   The first non-flat foot patch occurs BEFORE separation: left foot tick 383, right foot 390
   (active patch collapses from the full sole -0.219..0.084 m to a single mid-sole line);
   separation is at 396. The ankle regulator reaches its +/-4 deg clamp at tick 380 (left)/386.
   Cause: during Pulling the A3 pretension servo, whose setpoint is 275.9 N per hand, treats the
   player's pull as a disturbance. Measured hand load rises 270 -> 320-457 N, so the servo drives
   the reference hand load DOWN: H_ref 275.9 (t300) -> 210.6 (t340) -> 117.8 (t380) -> 69.7 (t396).
   The contact-consistent ankle feedforward follows H_ref (61.3 -> 20.9 N m) while the same C4
   statics at the MEASURED hand load require 65.8 -> 96.8 N m. The 40-76 N m deficit is absorbed by
   the ankle regulator until it saturates, the sole COP runs to an edge and the feet rock.
   The airborne schedule then starts from the H = 275.9 N grounded endpoint, not from the applied
   60-70 N reference: the +44 N m ankle-reference step lands on an already rocking foot (toe edge
   ticks 410-470, heel edge from ~484, backward fall over the heels).
   Momentum reconstruction (Tools/Experiments/Analyze-GAM70MomentumBalance.py): vertical
   linear-momentum residual of athlete+bar <= 2.5 N through tick 679 (instrumentation closes);
   sagittal angular momentum about the system COM reaches 27.85 kg m^2/s; inferred sole friction
   1373 N at the collapse. Largest segment angular accelerations at tick 399: thighs 14-25 rad/s^2.
   Static B2 moments were not used as a dynamic solution: the deficit is evaluated against the
   statics at the realized hand load, and the failure is attributed to the reference, not to
   joint capacity (ankle X demand at first patch loss 0.95 x 450 N m is a regulator response).
