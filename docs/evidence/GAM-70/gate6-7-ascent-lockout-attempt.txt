GAM-70 Gates 6-7 - moving phase: ascent, knee passage, lockout, Down, lowering, decision
2026-11-16, Unity 6000.3.22f1, fresh B2 plants, 100 kg athlete, real 75 kg bar, both rigid grips,
deadlift ankle-roll DOF. No gain, strength, stiffness, solver, friction, contact or joint-limit
value was changed. The Gate 5 measured hand-load reference (re-solved at the current posture)
is the static reference throughout; PoweredJointController is the only joint writer.

1. Reference path (DeadliftLiftPath, kinematic, written before tick 0)
   Keys over the athlete's real joints/anchors/limits (setup-solver parameterization):
   hard - feet at their setup poses, both palm anchors on the level bar with the setup hand
   orientations (welded grips), joint ranges with 3 deg margin, bar-shin/thigh clearance,
   arm/leg non-overlap, athlete+bar COM inside the sole hull with 60 mm margin, knees >= 4 deg
   short of their stop, shoulder static moment (hand load W_b/2 + arm weight) <= 0.85 of the
   130 N m capacity, and (free-rise keys) no bar descent (IPF 4.3.1(1));
   preference - bar rising vertically, torso held at the setup angle until the bar clears the
   knee (knee passage rise 0.457 m, from the rig geometry) then extended to 86 deg, shins to
   vertical before passage, thighs to vertical after, hands under shoulders, neutral spine.
   Bar-rise sweep at 1 cm, then a 12-step homotopy into the lockout (every key closed).
   Result: 91 keys, lockout rise 0.792 m, sweep hard cost <= 2e-2 (mm-level foot/hand
   residuals), lockout key 0.32 (shoulders-back/feet terms, few mm). Solve 12-17 s.
   Progress s is the joint-space arc length (largest single-joint rotation), so a constant
   rate is a bounded joint speed.

   a. First path (torso schedule linear in bar rise): the solution family jumped between
      0.15-0.30 m (hard cost 1.26, bar into the shins); ascent tracked to +151 mm then the
      abdomen saturated (2.97). -> knee-passage-first coordination.
   b. Bar-rise parameterization: knee reference snapped 16 -> 0.7 deg in ~9 ticks
      (~170 deg/s), hit the -5 deg stop, both feet left the platform (ticks 629-635).
      -> joint arc-length parameterization and a soft knee floor.
   c. Slerp between the last swept key and a separately solved lockout is not closed: a
      planted foot was asked to rotate (COP 0.08 m toe edge, ankle 38 vs 13 deg).
      -> closed homotopy keys with monotone bar rise.
   d. Instant progress stop (Drive release): one foot unloaded (0/2472 N) within 0.4 s.
      -> progress rate with bounded acceleration (0.6 /s^2) and a stopping-distance arrival.
      Control L3 (Drive released at s 0.60): bar +640 mm held motionless 4 s, feet 861/855 N,
      margin >= 115 mm: the standing-height balance loop is statically sound.
   e. Regulator integral: measured COP 10-18 mm ahead of the quasi-static target from s 0.33;
      the pure integral wound to its -4 deg clamp (~2 s) and threw the COP to the toe edge.
      -> moving phase integral leak 0.1 s (Kp, Ki, clamps unchanged; hold law unchanged).
   f. Regulator outer term used the athlete-only COM velocity (-0.06..-0.09 m/s) while the
      athlete+bar COM stood still; target biased 20-30 mm. A/B L1 (athlete) loses both feet at
      685-688; L2 (athlete+bar, the only difference) reaches lockout, no contact loss.
   g. Lockout key needed ~100 % shoulder capacity (hands ahead of the shoulders): shoulder
      saturated (1.06-1.25) and the bar limit-cycled 35 mm. -> shoulder static-moment bound.
   h. Lockout planned 49 mm inside the heel edge tipped backward on arrival. -> 60 mm margin,
      torso 86 deg (referee minimum 80), shoulders-back kept hard.
   The legacy COM-displacement governor never acts in the moving phase; progress is held,
   never forced, by the capture-point margin (DeadliftSupportMonitor, >= 40 mm) or a joint
   reference demand above 0.95.
