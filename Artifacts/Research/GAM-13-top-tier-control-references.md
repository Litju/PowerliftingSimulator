# GAM-13 audited reference set

Purpose: support the identification/control methodology and the qualitative
sticking-region context. These sources do **not** validate the Unity plant,
its magnitudes, its actuator model, or its same-machine results.

## System identification and control

1. **Forssell, U.; Ljung, L. (1999).** “Closed-loop Identification
   Revisited.” *Automatica*, 35(7), 1215–1241. Original methodological
   research. DOI: [10.1016/S0005-1098(99)00022-9](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0005-1098(99)00022-9).
   Use: regulator-dependent bias and closed-loop identification validity.

2. **Van Overschee, P.; De Moor, B. (1997).** “Closed-Loop Subspace System
   Identification.” *Proceedings of the 36th IEEE Conference on Decision and
   Control*. Original methodological research. DOI:
   [10.1109/CDC.1997.657851](https://doi.org/10.1109/CDC.1997.657851).
   Use: MIMO/state-space identification from closed-loop input/output data.

3. **Schoukens, J.; Ljung, L. (2019).** “Nonlinear System Identification: A
   User-Oriented Road Map.” *IEEE Control Systems*, 39(6), 28–99.
   Peer-reviewed methodological roadmap. DOI:
   [10.1109/MCS.2019.2938121](https://doi.org/10.1109/MCS.2019.2938121).
   Use: local validity regions, excitation design, nonlinear-model limits, and
   separate validation data.

4. **Doyle, J. C.; Glover, K.; Khargonekar, P. P.; Francis, B. A. (1989).**
   “State-Space Solutions to Standard H2 and H-infinity Control Problems.”
   *IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control*, 34(8), 831–847. Primary robust
   control theory. DOI:
   [10.1109/9.29425](https://doi.org/10.1109/9.29425).
   Use: robust synthesis requires closed-loop stability/performance conditions,
   not a nonzero static determinant.

## Squat sticking/failure context

5. **Larsen, S.; Kristiansen, E.; van den Tillaar, R. (2021).** “New Insights
   About the Sticking Region in Back Squats: An Analysis of Kinematics,
   Kinetics, and Myoelectric Activity.” *Frontiers in Sports and Active
   Living*, 3:691459. Original research. DOI:
   [10.3389/fspor.2021.691459](https://doi.org/10.3389/fspor.2021.691459).
   Use: event structure of a sticking region in successful 3-RM back squats.

6. **van den Tillaar, R.; Andersen, V.; Sæterbakken, A. H. (2014).** “The
   Existence of a Sticking Region in Free Weight Squats.” *Journal of Human
   Kinetics*, 42, 63–71. Original research. DOI:
   [10.2478/hukin-2014-0061](https://doi.org/10.2478/hukin-2014-0061).
   Use: sticking was observed in only a subset of participants; absence is not
   automatically a defect.

7. **Larsen, S.; Kristiansen, E.; van den Tillaar, R. (2022).** “Effects of
   Barbell Load on Kinematics, Kinetics, and Myoelectric Activity in Back
   Squats.” *Sports Biomechanics*. Original research. DOI:
   [10.1080/14763141.2022.2085164](https://doi.org/10.1080/14763141.2022.2085164).
   Use: qualitative ordering across 90%, 100%, and failed 102% 1-RM attempts.

## Citation boundaries

- The first four sources justify the report's system-identification and robust-
  control distinctions; they are not measurements of this game.
- The final three sources support only qualitative human event framing. Their
  participant populations, 1-RM normalization, EMG, and inverse-dynamics
  results are not imported as game parameters.
- Blogs, aggregator summaries, and search-result pages are not used as
  evidence.
